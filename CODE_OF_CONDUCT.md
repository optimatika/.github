# Code of Conduct

Just be nice.

That is the short version, and for almost everyone it is the whole of it. The rest of this page exists so that anyone deciding whether to report something knows what to expect.

## What that means in practice

Keep the discussion about the code. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticism of a design decision is welcome and expected, and a minimal reproducer settles an argument faster than an adjective does. Personal attacks, harassment and sustained disruption are not welcome, and neither is publishing anyone's private information.

Not everyone here works in their first language, and not everyone has the same background in mathematics or in Java. Impatience with that is the main way an open source project turns unpleasant.

## Where it applies

Everywhere the projects live: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, the wiki, and anywhere someone is representing an Optimatika project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainers through the form at www.optimatika.se/contact. Reports are handled privately, and we will not pass on who reported what.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock a thread, or block an account. We would much rather have a quiet word first, and normally that is enough.
