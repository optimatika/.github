# Support

ojAlgo and the other Optimatika projects are open source under the MIT licence. You may use
them for anything, also in closed-source commercial products, without asking.

Open source works when it goes both ways. This is how it works here.

## Found a bug? Open an issue

Anyone can, and every credible report is looked into. Open an issue on the repository
concerned – for the Optimisation Service, server or client, that is
[optimisation-service-client](https://github.com/optimatika/optimisation-service-client/issues).
Say which version you are on, what you expected, and what happened instead, and include the
smallest program that shows it.

A report is not a promise of a fix, or of when. A reproducer is what most often turns one
into a fix.

## A question? Ask in Discussions – and give something back

Questions in [Discussions](https://github.com/optimatika/ojAlgo/discussions) are answered for
people who contribute. The bar is low, but there has to be something: a pull request, a test
case, a documentation fix, a good bug report, an answer to someone else's question – or an
open source project of your own that builds on ojAlgo. Say what it is, or link to it, when
you ask.

Search first: modelling, solver selection and numerical tolerances come up again and again,
and many questions already have answers. Documentation and articles are at
[ojalgo.org](https://www.ojalgo.org/).

Everything posted is public – including what it reveals about what you are building – and
stays that way.

## Part of your business? Pay for support

If ojAlgo is part of something you sell or run as a service, pay for support – whatever the
size of the business, and whether or not it makes money yet. If your own code is worth
keeping closed, the code it builds on is worth paying for. Someone pays for ojAlgo's
development and maintenance; otherwise you leave that to others.

[Optimatika](https://www.optimatika.se/support/), the company behind ojAlgo, sells it as a
subscription: private questions and answers, with a response time. Support is answers – it
never includes writing code, not even bug fixes. Every case starts with the
[contact form](https://www.optimatika.se/contact/). The response time applies to cases
started there, not to questions asked here.

## Something looks like a security problem?

Do not open an issue. Follow the
[security policy](https://github.com/optimatika/.github/blob/master/SECURITY.md) instead.

## The licence is not affected

There is no commercial edition and no dual licence. Paying for support changes nothing
about the terms you already have.
