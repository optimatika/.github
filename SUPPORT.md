 # Support

 ## How do I use this?

 Ask in Discussions on the repository concerned. Search the existing threads first, since questions about modelling, solver selection and numerical tolerances tend to recur and many already have answers. Documentation and articles are at www.ojalgo.org, and the wiki holds the longer worked examples.

 ## Something is broken

 Open an issue on the repository concerned. Say which version you are on, what you expected, and what happened instead, and include the smallest program that shows it. A model that actually fails is worth far more than a description of one.

 ## Something looks like a security problem

 Do not open an issue. Follow the security policy instead.

 ## Commercial support

 Optimatika offers paid support to organisations whose code depends on ojAlgo: help with modelling, performance work, and direct access to the people who develop the library. It is an agreement about expertise rather than a transfer of risk, and it comes with no warranty. Details and enquiries at www.optimatika.se.

 ojAlgo stays open source under the MIT licence. There is no commercial edition and no dual licence; paid support changes nothing about the terms you already have.
